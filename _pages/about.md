I am a PhD candidate in Economics at York University. 

Coming from a developing country, I am interested in understanding how these economies can achieve sustained growth by making better use of their existing resources. My current research focuses on macroeconomic development and misallocation, particularly on how financial frictions and government policies shape resource allocation and aggregate productivity. My work combines quantitative modeling, disciplined by micro-level data, with empirical analysis to study the productive potential of developing economies.

I am on the 2026–27 job market and open to opportunities in academia and the public and private sectors.

**Research interests:** Macroeconomics, Misallocation, Financial Frictions, Economic Development and Growth. 

**Job Market Paper:** [Size-dependent Subsidies and Financial Frictions: A Quantitative Analysis in Vietnam]
