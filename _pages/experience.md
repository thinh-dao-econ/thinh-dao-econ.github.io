Before and alongside my PhD, I worked in research, teaching, international development, and banking. Most of this work involved building and analysing large datasets, which shaped my research on misallocation and financial frictions in Vietnam.

## Research and teaching at York University

**Research Assistant to Professor Tasso Adamopoulos** (2023–2025)
I prepared and analysed household survey data from the World Bank's Living Standards Measurement Study for several Sub-Saharan African countries (typically 3,000 to 10,000 households per country and survey wave). My work included building household-level and district-level datasets, constructing income, consumption, land and food-security measures, linking survey waves into panels, computing inequality statistics, producing graphs and regression output, and documenting every step in R. The projects covered rural inequality and food security.

**Research Assistant to Professor Jun Zhao** (2022–2024)
I supported a study of Canada's federal Procurement Strategy for Aboriginal Business. I wrote a Python pipeline that collected more than 100,000 award notices and related tender records from the public CanadaBuys website, cleaned and matched them to the federal contract history (about 460,000 contract records, 2009–2022), and built the analysis samples. In R, I computed summary statistics, geocoded buyers and suppliers, ran exploratory regressions and wrote regular reports.

**Teaching Assistant, Department of Economics** (2021–present)
I have been a teaching assistant for 14 courses across macroeconomics, international trade, quantitative methods and microeconomics. In the large introductory courses ECON 1000 and ECON 1010 (more than 1,000 students per term), I served as the administrative TA: I managed the course's online portal, compiled grades from the other TAs, and tested new platform features. See the [Teaching page]({{ site.baseurl }}/teaching/) for the course list.

## International development

**Short-Term Consultant, World Bank Group, Hanoi, Vietnam** (2020 and 2021)
- *Data analysis (February to June 2020).* I helped design a standardized set of statistical templates (about 180 tables in 15 workbooks) for Vietnam Social Security, building on a World Bank template, and revised them after comments from the International Labour Organization. I wrote an Excel macro to organize them, summarized meetings with the counterpart agencies, and ran logical-consistency checks on the data collected.
- *Pension data analysis and policy support (April to June 2021).* I commented on and analysed pension projection materials produced by the World Bank and the ILO, and contributed the data analysis for policy recommendations that I explained to Vietnam's labor ministry.

## Banking

**Bank for Investment and Development of Vietnam (BIDV)** (2012–2020)
- *Human resources (2015–2020).* I analysed branch performance to set staffing needs, ran recruitment (about 5,000 applications screened and about 1,000 hires a year across roughly 220 branches), contributed to the design of the bank's recruitment software, and helped plan staffing for a large bank merger.
- *Business planning (2013–2015).* I developed business plans and performance evaluations for the bank and its branches, and proposed a restructuring plan for an underperforming regional branch, approved by the bank's Board of Directors.
- *Corporate credit analysis (2012–2013).* I analysed corporate clients' financial statements and collateral and recommended credit terms.

My experience with lending and interest-rate regulation for small and medium-sized firms in Vietnam is one of the motivations for my dissertation research.
