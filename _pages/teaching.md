## Teaching assistant, Department of Economics, York University

I have been a teaching assistant at York University since Fall 2021, across 14 different courses in macroeconomics, international trade, quantitative methods and microeconomics. In most courses my main role is grading assignments and exams. In the two largest introductory courses, ECON 1000 and ECON 1010, I served as Head TA. In ECON 2500 I also lead tutorials, where I explain course material and work through exercise solutions with students.

## Head TA, ECON 1000 and ECON 1010

These are large multi-section courses, with more than 1,000 students in a term (more than 2,000 in some terms). Grades and course materials are managed together on one online portal integrated with York's eClass platform. As Head TA I:

- managed the online portal, including tracing student activity logs for quizzes and online assignments when technical problems occurred;
- compiled grades from the other TAs, confirmed them with the course directors, and uploaded final grades for all students;
- tested new features built on the online platform before they were released.
